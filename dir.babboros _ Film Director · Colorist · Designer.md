## Mugerwa Othman, aka dir.babboros

dir.babboros is the professional alias and social media handle of Mugerwa Othman, a Ugandan film director, video editor, colorist and director of photography based in Kampala.

He is the founder of **Iconeon Studios**, a creative pre- and post-production company in Africa that handles scripting, filming and video editing. Beyond directing, he works as a motion graphic designer, colorist, editor and online lecturer.
